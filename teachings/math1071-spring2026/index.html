MATH 1071: Introduction to Discrete Mathematics

Logic, sets, methods of proof, induction, functions, counting and probability, and graph theory, for students in information technology and programming.

Term: Spring

Role: Instructor of record, Section 001
Format: In person, three days a week
Textbook: Discrete Mathematics with Applications (5th ed.), Epp, with WebAssign

Assessment structure

  • Weekly online homework and weekly quizzes, both open to collaboration
  • Three in-class exams and a cumulative final exam
