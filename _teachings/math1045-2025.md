**Role:** Instructor of record (Spring 2025; Fall 2025, Section 001)<br>
**Format:** In person, flipped classroom<br>
**Textbook:** *Calculus Concepts* (5th ed.), LaTorre et al.

MATH 1045 is a large, coordinated course with common tests and a common final exam across sections. In Fall 2025 it used a flipped format: students watched lecture videos before class, and class time was spent working problems in groups while I circulated and answered questions.

**Assessment structure**

- Three in-person chapter tests, each with one retake, and a common final exam
- Pre-class quizzes on each lecture video and in-class participation quizzes
- Two technology projects applying calculus, each with one resubmission
