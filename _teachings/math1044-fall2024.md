**Role:** Instructor of record, Section 011<br>
**Format:** In person, three days a week<br>
**Textbook:** *Calculus Concepts* (5th ed.), LaTorre et al.

MATH 1044 is a large, coordinated course. Assessments and course policies are shared across all sections, and each instructor teaches and runs their own section.

**Assessment structure**

- Six in-person Content Checks, each with one re-attempt
- A midterm exam and a common final exam
- Online homework and participation quizzes
- A diagnostic test in Week 1 to identify students who could benefit from supplemental review sessions
