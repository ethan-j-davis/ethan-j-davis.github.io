**Role:** Instructor of record, Section 002<br>
**Format:** In person, seven-week summer session (two-hour class meetings, three days a week)<br>
**Textbook:** *College Algebra Workbook* (V2.2) and *College Algebra* (6th ed.), Rockswold

A typical class covered two workbook sections and included a short check-in on the material from the previous class, with remaining time for assignments.

**Assessment structure**

- Check-ins at each class meeting
- Four quizzes, three exams, and a comprehensive final exam
- Homework
