**Role:** Instructor of record, Section 003<br>
**Format:** Asynchronous online, 13-week summer session<br>
**Textbook:** *Calculus: Early Transcendentals* (9th ed.), Stewart, with WebAssign

Students worked through lecture videos for each section on a recommended weekly pace, with a Monday announcement outlining the focus of each week.

**Assessment structure**

- Online homework due three times a week
- Weekly timed quizzes with handwritten work uploaded for grading
- Two proctored online exams and a proctored cumulative final exam
