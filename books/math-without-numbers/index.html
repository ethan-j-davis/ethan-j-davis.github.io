Math Without Numbers

Milo Beckman  ·  2021

 ·  topology   infinity  ·  popular-math


Milo Beckman gives an illustrated, conversational tour of the three main branches of abstract math (topology, analysis, and algebra) with hardly an equation in sight. Chapters work through shape and dimension, the infinite and the infinitesimal, symmetry, and proof, with questions like how many shapes there are and whether anything is bigger than infinity. His larger argument is that math is mostly about recognizing patterns, and that its big ideas are within reach of any curious reader.
