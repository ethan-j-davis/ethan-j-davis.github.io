Have you ever read Romeo and Juliet with the Shakespearean language on the left and the modern day translation on the right? That is what this book reminds me of.

I feel like every mathematician has had some sort of internal crisis about the Incompleteness Theorems. This book helped demystify the surprisingly complex formal logic of the proofs.
